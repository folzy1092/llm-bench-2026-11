Independent data agrees with Anthropic's table: the AA index is 58 for Opus versus 49 for Fable. On GDPval-AA Opus also leads: 1846 vs 1735 Elo. Fable also costs more per token: $10 / $50 versus $4 / $20 dollars per million input / output tokens.

Two caveats. Anthropic itself says the gap is narrower in practice than the numbers suggest and advises starting with Opus, moving to Fable only if Opus cannot finish a long task. And Opus was tested with safeguards on: cyber and bio tasks are routed to an older model, which lowers its scores.

What to pick

For typical developer work: Python backend, bots, frontend.

Python backend

Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code at medium effort. It leads FrontierCode and Terminal-Bench, and practitioners report less rework on existing code. GPT-6.1 Sol is fine for routine work.

Telegram bots

Usually simple logic plus integrations, so frontier-level intelligence is overkill. Pick GPT-6.1 Sol and bring in Opus for tricky handlers and architecture. If the bot takes voice messages, note that Opus has no audio input. No head-to-head comparison of the three models on bots specifically could be found.

Frontend

Opus is first on WebDev Arena: 1810 Elo versus 1773 for Astra. Practitioners praise Astra for quick new components. Argon looks strong on paper but is unstable in hands-on tests.

Price and quality

GPT-6.1 Sol is the best balance: 55 index points at $1.27 per task. Kimi K3.1 Pro fits if you need open weights. Skip Fable 5.1 and Grok 4.7: Grok's cheap tokens are eaten up by how many of them it uses.

FAQ

Which AI model is the best in November 2026?

Among publicly available models, Claude Opus 5.5 leads the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 with 58 points, just ahead of GPT-6 Astra (57) and GPT-6.1 Sol (55). Gemini 4 Carbon scores 59, but only cyber defenders can access it.

Is Claude Fable 5.1 worse than Opus 5.5?

In Anthropic's own table Opus 5.5 is ahead in all 9 comparable tests, and the independent index agrees (58 vs 49). Fable also costs more per token: $10 / $50 versus $4 / $20 dollars per million input / output tokens. Anthropic itself says the gap is narrower in practice.

Which model is best for coding?

Claude Opus 5.5 leads on agentic coding and frontend (Terminal-Bench, FrontierCode, Arena WebDev). GPT-6.1 Sol offers the best price-to-quality ratio: 55 index points at $1.27 per index task versus $10.04 for Opus. Gemini 4 Argon is strong at long-context work.

Does Gemini 3.8 Pro exist?

No. There are Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.1 Pro (preview). Google's current flagship is Gemini 4 Argon; the stronger Gemini 4 Carbon is closed to the public.

Can I trust vendor benchmark numbers?

Treat them with care. The same test can differ by more than ten points between vendor and independent runs: for Grok 4.7 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, xAI reports 37.6% while Artificial Analysis measured 25.8%.

Caveats

  • Most numbers come from the vendors' own tables, with settings that suit them. The same test in a different harness differs by more than ten points, as the Terminal-Bench chart shows.
  • The AA v5 index is brand new and shifted scores noticeably. Do not mix older v4 comparisons with it.
  • AA speed varies between measurements and the method changed for some models. Treat speed figures as approximate.
  • Practitioner reviews are single runs and blog posts: a signal, not statistics.
  • Not found: ARC-AGI-3 for Opus, Fable, Grok, Kimi and GLM; v5 index for Grok 4.7; WebDev Elo for Argon; MMMU and AIME for this generation.
  • License and actual release of the Kimi K3.1 Pro weights were not confirmed at the time of data collection.
  • This page was assembled from an automated research pass over public sources. Verify key numbers against the linked sources before relying on them.
